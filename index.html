About ANANDAM

The story of our home and the name we gave it.

Why “Anandam”?

Anandam means joy and bliss in Sanskrit. We chose it because a home is happiest when many generations share it — grandparents’ blessings, parents’ effort and children’s laughter under one roof.

The tree in our logo stands for our roots and growing branches. The rising sun is a new day together. The lotus is the peace we hope every visitor feels at our door.

“A house is built with bricks, but a home is built with love, respect and time spent together.”

Our Mission

To keep our family bound by love, to honour our elders, and to pass our stories, values and traditions to every new generation.

Our Vision

A home where every member feels respected and heard — and where each generation is proud to say, “this is where I belong.”
